
    These notes are meant to add structure to a first course in discrete mathematics; these do not represent a full coverage of the content.  The class these are used in has a prerequisite of Precalculus partly for content but also for mathematical maturity.  The prerequisites mean that the course difficulty is on par with Calculus 1 however, the nature of the material in the course is much different.  While algebra is used in and needed for this course the thinking used is more frequently logical or qualitative rather than quantitative or algebraic. For this reason students will need to be particularly diligent since this way of thinking will likely be new to them. The material in this set of notes is based largely, but not exclusively on material in \textit{Discrete Mathematics with Applications} by Epp\cite{epp2019discrete}.  There will on occasion also be material pulled from discrete mathematics textbooks by Rosen \cite{rosen2006discrete} and Grimaldi \cite{grimaldi1994discrete}.
